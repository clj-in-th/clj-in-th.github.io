(clj-in-th)
The Home of the Clojure Community in Thailand

Who is this for?

Anyone who is interested in Clojure or Functional Programming is welcome regardless of experience or professional pedigree. Anyone who cares about quality and craftsmanship. clj-in-th is just as much for the newcomer to the language as it is for Clojure engineers in Thailand to have a place to socialise.

What do you do?

We currently meet monthly, either in a video call, or in person when circumstances permit. The meetings serve as a platform both for hosting speakers, and to provide a space for community-led projects for anyone interested in developing their skills in Clojure, whether they are completely unfamiliar with it or have been building enterprise Clojure for years. The community is also intended to facilitate local meetups and impropmptu events amongst community members.

How do I join?

Simply drop into our Discord Server linked below to say "Hello"!

What is Clojure?

Clojure is a modern functional lisp dialect targeting the JVM, Javascript, and .NET runtimes, can be compiled to native binaries via GraalVM, C++, or Chez Scheme, as well as being able to interoperate with python libraries. Additionally Clojure can be used as a terminal scripting language via Babashka. It provides an interactive developer experience, allowing dynamic reloading of code at runtime. Combined with the ability to manipulate the structure of the code directly and evaluate it in the editor via integrated REPL tooling, this enables an unparelled level of transparency in the construction and examination of complex systems.

Why Functional Programming?

A functional approach to software design enables simpler and more composable architectures. Software can be conceptually more ergonomic to build and use without compromising computational efficiency.
